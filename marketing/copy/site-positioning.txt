# FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

## Is StaffAi just another chatbot?

No.

Conversation is simply one way you communicate with your company.

StaffAi employees have defined jobs, company context, tools, permissions, managers and responsibilities. Within their authority, they perform real business work rather than simply generating answers.

## Do I need to understand AI?

No.

You don't need to choose models, write prompts, manage tokens, configure agents or understand AI infrastructure.

Tell your company what you need in ordinary business language.

## Do I have to replace my existing software?

Not necessarily.

StaffAi is designed to work with supported business systems where appropriate. If something already works for your company, there is no reason to replace it merely for the sake of replacing it.

## Can employees do things without asking me every time?

Yes, for routine work you've authorized.

StaffAi uses balanced autonomy by default. Everyday authorized work can proceed while consequential or high-risk actions require approval.

## What if an employee makes a mistake?

AI isn't infallible, and we won't pretend it is.

StaffAi uses defined responsibilities, permissions, approval rules, activity history and escalation to reduce risk. Employees can be paused, permissions changed and issues corrected when necessary.

## Can StaffAi work with my existing employees?

Yes.

StaffAi employees can work alongside human staff and hand work to them when appropriate.

## Is my company information shared with other StaffAi companies?

No.

Your company operates within its own tenant environment with access controls designed to keep company information separated.

## Can my accountant access my financial records?

Controlled access can be provided to authorized professionals where supported, allowing your accountant to work with the appropriate company records without giving unnecessary access to the rest of your organization.

## How much can an employee do?

StaffAi plans include generous capacity for normal business use.

One AI employee can handle substantial concurrent work and doesn't need to be duplicated simply because several tasks happen at once.

Businesses with unusually high calling, messaging or campaign volumes can add capacity as needed. Pricing is discussed in advance rather than appearing as surprise usage charges.

## Can I cancel?

Yes.

Company Office and employee subscriptions are month-to-month unless you choose annual billing.

## What happens in your first week?

We establish your Company Office immediately. Your Executive Assistant and General Manager learn your business and start working the same day.

The goal is not to show you another empty dashboard.

It's to let you experience what it feels like to delegate work to your company.
