# STAFF AI: PHASE 2 INFRASTRUCTURE EXPANSION

**Target:** Post-Launch / Scaling Phase
**Focus:** Autonomous Agents & Enterprise RAG Knowledge Integration

## 1. DeerFlow (The Autonomous Agent Engine)
**What it is:** An open-source "SuperAgent harness" developed by ByteDance that gives AI agents a "computer-in-a-box" (a secure Docker environment with a bash terminal, file system, and coding capabilities).
**StaffAi Integration:**
*   Move beyond standard "chatbots" to leasing out **Autonomous Digital Employees**.
*   Instead of an AI that just answers FAQs, DeerFlow allows StaffAi to deploy "AI Data Analysts" or "AI Researchers" that can be given a prompt and work completely autonomously for 8+ hours in their own virtual environment to produce a final deliverable (like a slide deck or a research report).

## 2. Onyx (The Enterprise Brain / RAG)
**What it is:** An open-source Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) platform designed for enterprise search and workflow automation.
**StaffAi Integration:**
*   **The Onboarding Solution:** Onyx will serve as the backend knowledge layer for StaffAi. 
*   When Kenetix sells a high-ticket B2B StaffAi package, the client simply connects their internal tools (Google Drive, Slack, Zendesk, Asana) to Onyx.
*   Onyx instantly ingests the entire company history, policies, and product documentation, allowing the StaffAi agents to immediately act with deep, company-specific context. This completely removes the friction of "training" the AI and justifies premium pricing.
