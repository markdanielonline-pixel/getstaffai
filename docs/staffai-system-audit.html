01 · How to read this

Every claim carries a label saying how it was established. The labels are the point of the document. A page of confident prose is exactly what cannot be trusted; a page that marks its own weak spots can be.

Verified means it was executed against production and observed. A database row, an API response, a screenshot, a log line. If an investor's developer challenges it, there is evidence.

Traced means the code path was read end to end and can be cited, but was not run. Honest, and weaker. Most things labelled this way are true; the point is that nobody has proved it.

Not proven means it could not be established, usually because it needs a real card, a real customer, or a service not yet connected. Do not repeat these as fact.

Why the labels exist. On 7 September the product was described as ready to launch on the strength of component checks: the runtime was pinned, the agents were active, delegation worked. All of that was true. Then the first attempt to use the product as a customer found the password reset broken, then signup, then checkout, then the ability to message an assistant. Components were healthy; the journeys were impassable. Everything below was therefore established by walking the journey, not by reading the parts.

02 · Findings

Thirteen defects, found across roughly a dozen paths. Eleven are fixed and deployed; two are decisions rather than bugs. Severity is by commercial consequence, not by how hard it was to fix.

Fixed and verified

Nobody could create an account Verified

Confirmation emails pointed at a server route that looks for a ?code= parameter. Supabase returns the session in the URL fragment instead, which is never transmitted to a server. Every new customer who clicked confirm landed on /portal/login?error=auth_callback_failed.

Fixed: a client page at /auth/finish now consumes either shape. Proved by creating a throwaway account and following a real confirmation link to a signed-in onboarding screen.

Nobody could buy anything Verified

All 24 Stripe price identifiers were empty strings in production, so checkout answered “This plan is not currently available” for every product and period. The only populated prices belonged to a retired tier structure. This is why the subscriptions table and the entire Stripe account held zero subscriptions.

Fixed: all 24 wired to their existing Stripe prices, which already matched the catalogue to the cent. Proved by generating a live checkout session, cs_live_b1EHFsp7…

Every employee after the first two was free Verified

Hiring recorded a seat fee, displayed a monthly price beside the button, and made no Stripe call whatsoever. Stripe was touched in exactly three files and hiring was not one of them. A customer paying $199 could staff an entire company at no additional charge.

Fixed: a seat is now a prorated line on the customer's subscription, charged before the runtime is built, refunded if the hire then fails.

The customer could not message their assistant Verified

The dashboard applied the 280px sidebar offset twice, so the conversation screen ran 560px past the window and the Send button sat off-screen. Where it was reachable, the support chat bubble covered it. Confirmed from the page itself: elementFromPoint at the button's centre returned a different button.

Fixed: both offsets corrected. Proved with a real mouse click that landed a message in the database and drew a reply from Sophia.

Password reset could not complete Verified

Same fragment-versus-code fault, plus a reset page that rendered a complete, believable password form whether or not the link had signed you in. Reset mail also came from noreply@mail.app.supabase.io.

Fixed: reset mail is now generated and sent by Staff AI through Resend from its own domain, and the link verifies server-side so it survives being opened on a different device from the one that requested it.

Starting a checkout rewrote a live company Verified

Checkout wrote its form values over the organisation name, industry and business description before any payment. Abandoning checkout left the changes in place. Found by doing it: a test renamed a tenant with eight working employees and replaced the description its staff work from.

Fixed: an organisation with staff is treated as established; checkout sets the active organisation and nothing else.

Dismissal destroyed an employee in one click Verified

No confirmation, no warning, no undo. The runtime is decommissioned and the employee marked alumni; hiring the role again builds a different person. Found because you clicked it to learn what it meant.

Fixed: a confirmation step that names the person, states plainly that it cannot be undone, and takes an optional reason.

A failed hire could charge and deliver nothing Verified

Introduced by the seat-billing fix and caught in the same session. The charge precedes provisioning deliberately, but nothing reversed it if provisioning then failed.

Fixed: creation is wrapped; a failure reverses the charge and records whether the reversal succeeded.

The navigation was unreadable Verified

Sidebar and header paint themselves near-black then asked the light theme for its text colour, which is near-black ink.

Fixed: dark surfaces define their own text tokens. Found the header by grep rather than waiting for you to report it.

The dashboard denied a working workforce Verified

Readiness depends on one record, initial-workforce:v1, which was stuck in running though its contents were complete and both agents verified active. The banner also re-triggered provisioning every sixty seconds against a healthy tenant.

Fixed: record corrected; the screen now reports the truth.

Billing screen showed a plan that did not exist Verified

Level and status fell back to stale fields, so an account that has never bought anything displayed Executive and Active. A cancelled customer would have been told they were active in the one place they would check.

Fixed: both read from entitlement, the same source the sidebar uses.

Open — decisions, not defects

One employee per role, per company Verified

A unique index on organisation, owner and role means a company cannot hire two Sales Representatives. Whether Staff AI sells multiple seats of the same role is a pricing decision. Until it is made, the error is at least stated in plain language rather than as a Postgres constraint name.

Direct employee chat still present Verified

The operating model says the CEO speaks only to the Executive Assistant. The Conversations screen still lists every employee as a separate thread. You have already asked for this to go; it has not been done.

04 · Signing up

  1. Visitor submits name, email and password on the signup page.app/portal/signup

  2. A Supabase auth user is created. If the address already exists, Supabase returns an obfuscated user with no identities rather than an error; the app detects this and redirects to sign-in instead of promising an email that will never arrive.app/actions/auth.js · signUp

  3. A CEO record is created by a database trigger, then the name is written to it.trigger handle_new_user → ceos

  4. Supabase sends the confirmation email. This one still comes from Supabase's own address, not your domain. Only password reset was moved to Resend.noreply@mail.app.supabase.io

  5. Clicking the link verifies the token and lands on a client page that reads the session out of the URL fragment and signs the person in.app/auth/finish

  6. They arrive at onboarding, signed in./portal/incorporate

Verified Steps 1 to 6, by creating two throwaway accounts and following real confirmation links, before and after the fix. Both accounts deleted afterwards.

Open: the signup confirmation email still comes from Supabase's shared domain. It works, but it is the first email a paying customer receives and it does not carry your name. Moving it to Resend is the same work already done for password reset.

05 · Password reset

  1. Customer enters an address and selects Forgot password.app/portal/login

  2. Staff AI asks Supabase for a recovery token, then composes and sends the email itself through Resend, from its own domain. Supabase's mailer is not used.lib/password-reset.js · lib/auth-email.js

  3. The reply is identical whether or not the address has an account, so the form cannot be used to discover customers.

  4. The link verifies server-side and sets a session cookie, which is why it works when requested on a laptop and opened on a phone.app/auth/confirm

  5. The reset page confirms a session exists before showing a password form. A link that signed nobody in says so and offers to send another.app/portal/reset-password

  6. The token is stripped from the address bar once used.

Verified End to end on the live site, including the cross-device case that originally caused the lockout.

06 · Buying

  1. Onboarding collects company name, industry, description, country and tone./portal/incorporate

  2. Checkout accepts the Company Office only. Individual roles are not sold here; they are hired later from inside the product.app/api/checkout

  3. An existing active, trialing or past-due subscription is refused with a message pointing at Billing.

  4. If the customer has no organisation one is created. If they have one with staff on it, its identity is left alone.

  5. A Stripe customer is created or reused, then a subscription-mode Checkout session. No trial. Paid from day one, sold on the 30-day guarantee.

  6. The customer is sent to Stripe to pay.

Verified Steps 1 to 6 produce a live Stripe session. Not proven Everything past the payment form. No card has ever been entered, so no subscription has ever existed.

What happens when payment succeeds

  1. Stripe calls the webhook. The event is stored before it is acted on, so a replay cannot double-process.app/api/webhooks/stripe

  2. The CEO is marked active with their plan level and subscription id.

  3. A subscription row and an empty wallet are created.

  4. Initial workforce provisioning begins: the Executive Assistant and General Manager.

Traced Read end to end and citable. Never executed, because it requires a real payment.

07 · Provisioning a workforce

  1. A durable operation record is opened so an interrupted run resumes rather than restarting.provisioning_operations · initial-workforce:v1

  2. Provision Core creates a team and a dedicated Docker container for the tenant.provision-runtime-<serverId>

  3. A dispatcher agent is installed, then the Executive Assistant, then the General Manager. Each install restarts the tenant's gateway.

  4. Each employee receives a written brief for its role, generated from the company's own name and description.lib/roles/playbooks.js

  5. Readiness is confirmed by checking the live team, both agents, and that they sit on the same server, with a short confirm window so a gateway restart is not mistaken for failure.

Verified Eight agents on the proof tenant, all active, confirmed both in the database and on the host itself.

Worth knowing. All tenants currently share one virtual machine. Every customer's agents run in their own container on that machine, isolated from one another, but a failure of that host is a failure for everyone. This is a capacity question, not a defect.

08 · Talking to the Executive Assistant

  1. The customer opens Conversations and writes to Sophia, their Executive Assistant./portal/dashboard/conversations

  2. The request is authenticated and the conversation confirmed to belong to that customer, so nobody can post into someone else's thread.app/api/employees/chat

  3. The message is recorded immediately, before any work starts.

  4. A task is dispatched to that employee's agent and the request waits for it, up to five minutes.

  5. If the employee is still busy with a previous request, the customer is told rather than queued silently.

  6. The reply is stored and returned. Work that finishes after the request has gone is reconciled and delivered later rather than lost.

Verified A message typed into the live site and sent with a real click reached the database, produced a task, and drew a written reply.

09 · Delegation

The chain the product is sold on: you speak only to the Executive Assistant, who hands work to the General Manager, who assigns a specialist.

Verified A request produced a real task assigned by the General Manager to the Lead Generation Specialist, who returned five independent Brooklyn plumbing companies, each site opened and confirmed live, with ownership evidence quoted from the companies' own pages.

A defect fixed along the way

Assignment by name used to fall through silently: a name that matched nothing created the task for the manager instead and answered success. The General Manager truthfully reported delegating work it still held. Names repeat in generated workforces, so this was not rare. An unresolvable or ambiguous assignee is now refused with the roster attached. Verified

10 · Hiring

  1. The customer picks a role. Roles whose advertised outcome cannot yet be delivered are listed as coming soon and cannot be hired.Bookkeeper, Receptionist

  2. The seat is charged first: a prorated line added to the existing subscription. Founder accounts are comped and recorded as such.lib/billing/seats.js

  3. Without an active subscription the hire is refused rather than quietly given away.

  4. The employee is created, a runtime agent installed, and a conversation opened so they can be reached.

  5. If anything fails after the charge, the charge is reversed and the outcome recorded either way.

  6. The seat is written to the billing ledger with its fee and Stripe line.

Verified A hire completed on the live site, agent reached active, seat recorded as comped. Duplicate-role refusal verified. Not proven The Stripe charge itself, which needs a paying subscription.

11 · Dismissal

  1. Dismiss opens a confirmation naming the person and stating that it cannot be undone.

  2. The Executive Assistant and General Manager cannot be dismissed; they are what the Company Office is.

  3. The runtime agent is decommissioned first, so nobody is marked departed while still answering.

  4. The seat charge is removed. A Stripe failure here does not block the dismissal; it is recorded instead.

  5. The employee becomes alumni with a reason and a departure time. Their conversations and past work remain readable.

Verified Confirmation step and refusal for the founding pair. Traced Seat release, which needs a billed seat to exercise.

Irreversible. Hiring the role again creates a new employee with a new identity, not the one removed. This is worth saying in the interface as well as here.

14 · Monitoring

Seven checks run every few minutes: the marketing site, the sales agent script, the sales agent itself with a live model call, the application, the Provision control plane, every customer workforce, and task delivery. Warnings cover stranded workforces, leftover tenants and unread customer reports.

A failing sweep is passed to a reliability engineer running on DeepSeek V4 Pro, which may take three actions from a fixed list, scoped to one organisation, and currently runs in dry-run: it proposes and records but does not act. Confirmed by inspection of its recorded incidents. Verified

Customer problem reports arrive through a control on every page, are rate limited, and reach the monitor as evidence. Report text is never treated as instruction to anything that can take action. Verified

16 · Data and isolation

Fifty-one tables, of which roughly half belong to earlier projects sharing the database and hold no rows. Row-level security is enabled on every table. Many carry no policy at all, which in Postgres means no access except through the service role — restrictive by default rather than permissive.

Tenant separation rests on the organisation identifier, and on membership: a customer can only activate an organisation they are recorded as a member of, so a tampered identifier cannot move them into another tenant. Traced

Recommended before investors look: the shared database holds tables from Beacon, Lynkwe and other projects. Nothing leaks, but it invites a question you would rather not spend the meeting answering.

18 · What is not proven

Say none of this as fact. It is the list to hand a technical diligence team so they hear it from you first.

  • No payment has ever been taken. Zero subscriptions exist on the Stripe account.
  • Upgrade, downgrade, cancellation and failed payment are read but never run.
  • Seat charging for a paying customer is unexercised; only the comped path has run.
  • Cancellation is believed not to stop a workforce. Untested.
  • Past-due access is believed to continue unrestricted. Untested.
  • Voice, telephony and the bookkeeping integrations are not connected.
  • Multi-user access within one company is untested; every account so far is a single owner.
  • No load beyond a handful of tenants on one host has been attempted.

All of it needs one thing: a real card entered once by a person. Everything downstream can then be driven and verified without touching a card again.

19 · Decisions only you can make

  1. Can a company hire two of the same role? Today it cannot. This is revenue either way, and the answer changes the pricing page.

  2. What happens when someone stops paying? Access and workforce both continue. Decide the grace period and what gets switched off, then it can be built.

  3. Remove direct employee chat? You have said yes. It is still there.

  4. Signup email from your domain? Small work, and it is the first thing a customer sees.

  5. Separate the database from the other projects? Not urgent, but it is a diligence question.

  6. One machine for all customers. Fine now. Decide the number of tenants at which that stops being fine.

Closing note

The engine is sound. The workforce provisions, the delegation chain runs end to end, and a specialist returned real verified work during this audit. Nothing found tonight was architectural.

What was broken was the wiring around it, and it was broken in the places nobody had walked: the front door, the checkout, the reply box. Three of the first four paths tested were impassable. That ratio is the argument for the method rather than for the conclusion.

The honest position is that the product now works for the journeys that have been walked, and is unproven for the journeys that have not. Both lists are above, and the second one shortens the moment a card goes in once.
